BASKETBALL RULES

The basics every fan and player should know

⏱️

Game Format

How It’s Played

5 players per team. NBA: 4 quarters of 12 minutes. FIBA / High School: 4 quarters of 10 / 8 minutes. Team with most points wins. Overtime if tied.

🎯

Scoring

2 • 3 • 1 Points

2 points for a shot inside the arc, 3 points outside the arc, 1 point for each free throw. A slam dunk counts as 2 points.

🏃

Dribbling & Movement

Keep The Ball Moving

You must dribble to move. No travelling (taking steps without dribbling), no double-dribble, no carrying the ball.

🕐

Shot Clock & Time

Play Fast

24-second shot clock in NBA/FIBA to attempt a shot. 8 seconds to bring the ball past half-court. 3 seconds max in the paint on offense.

🚫

Fouls

Play Clean

Personal fouls: illegal contact like pushing, holding, blocking. 6 fouls = foul out in NBA, 5 in FIBA. Team fouls lead to free throws.

⚖️

Violations

Turnover Rules

Out-of-bounds, goaltending, backcourt violation, shot-clock violation, and kicking the ball all give possession to the other team.

POSITIONS ON COURT

5 roles, one team

🧠

1. Point Guard

The Playmaker

Team leader. Best ball-handler and passer. Calls plays and sets up teammates. Example: Magic Johnson, Stephen Curry.

Skills: Passing | Speed | IQ
🔥

2. Shooting Guard

The Scorer

Best shooter on the team. Scores from mid-range and 3-point line. Good defender on the perimeter. Example: Michael Jordan, Kobe Bryant.

Skills: Shooting | Defense
🦅

3. Small Forward

The All-Rounder

Most versatile player. Scores, defends, rebounds. Can play inside and outside. Example: LeBron James, Kevin Durant.

Skills: Versatility | Athleticism
🛡️

4. Power Forward

The Enforcer

Strong inside scorer and rebounder. Screens, blocks shots, fights in the paint. Example: Tim Duncan, Giannis Antetokounmpo.

Skills: Rebounds | Strength
💪

5. Center

The Wall

Tallest player. Protects the rim, grabs rebounds, dunks and blocks. Anchors the defense. Example: Shaquille O’Neal, Nikola Jokić.

Skills: Blocks | Post Play
